# Walter — the walrus harbormaster. Gruff, kind, all moustache.

=== intro
!mumble walter ahoy!
!gesture walter Wave
@walter happy: Well, well! If it isn't little {name}, all grown up and standing on my pier!{p} Ahoy there, skipper!
@pip surprised: Walter! You haven't changed a bit. Is that the same moustache?
@walter smug: Same moustache, same walrus. I keep it trimmed for special occasions.{p} [happy]This is one.
@walter sad: Saltmoss has been {w}awfully quiet{/w} since your gran hung up her sou'wester.{p} The cannery's shut. Half the lanterns are dark.
@walter neutral: But she left you two things, and both of 'em still float. Mostly.
!gesture walter Point
@walter happy: The Sally Mae, tied up at the berth yonder.{p} And the Salty Puffin — her fish shop on the pier.
@pip determined: Then I'm going to fill that shop with the best catch on the Grey Sea!
@walter laugh: HAR! {s}That's the spirit!{/s}{p} [happy]Your gran said those exact words, forty years ago, on this exact plank.
@walter neutral: Go and say hello to Nell at the Salty Puffin. She's been keeping the counter warm.{p} Then come and see me before you take the boat out.
!flag intro_done

=== walter_first
!flag met_walter
@walter happy: There you are! Met Nell, did you? She'll have fed you three bowls of chowder.
@pip happy: Four. One was "a taster".
@walter laugh: HAR! That's Nell.{p} [neutral]Right then. The Sally Mae. She's old, she creaks, and she's the best crab boat on this coast.
@walter neutral: Steer with {c=#c24a3a}W A S D{/c} or the stick. Hold {c=#c24a3a}Shift{/c} to open her up. Toot the horn with {c=#c24a3a}H{/c} if you're feeling jolly.
@walter thinking: Out past the harbour mouth there's three grounds. The Shallows are gentle. Kelp Reach gets lively.{p} [worried]And the Grey Deep…{pp} the Grey Deep is where the king crab live. And the storms.
@walter neutral: Drop your crab pots when she's going slow, leave 'em to soak, then come back and haul.{p} Keep the winch needle in the green or the line just groans at you.
@walter happy: Cast a line at the fish shadows. Look for glimmers on the water — that's treasure down below. Old Inkwell will want anything shiny for his museum.
@walter smug: And if you see a rogue wave coming? Point her nose straight at it. Never take one on the side.
@walter happy: Off you go, skipper. The sea's been waiting for you.
!gesture walter Wave
