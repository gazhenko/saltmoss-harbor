# Nell — the sea otter who keeps the Salty Puffin's counter. Warm, chatty, thinks in chowder.

=== nell_hub
!if !flag:met_nell -> nell_first
!mumble nell
@nell happy: Hiya, {name}! What's cooking? {p}[laugh]Besides my chowder. Obviously.
? Open the shop -> nell_open | if stock>0
? Open the shop -> shop_empty | if stock==0
? Let's fix up the shop -> nell_upgrades
? Buy my junk? -> nell_junk
? How's business? -> nell_business
? Any chowder going? -> nell_chowder
? See you later! -> nell_bye

=== nell_first
!flag met_nell
!mumble nell oh hey!
!gesture nell Hop
@nell surprised: {b}{name}!{/b} Oh my whiskers, look at you! You've got your gran's beak!
@nell happy: I'm Nell. I've been minding the Salty Puffin since… well, since it stopped being a fish shop and started being a {w}very sad shed{/w}.
@nell worried: The ice trays are empty, the sign's peeling and the only customer this week was a gull asking for directions.
@pip determined: Not for long! I'm going to fill those trays.
@nell laugh: Ha! I like you already. Again!
@nell neutral: Here's how it works. Whatever you catch goes in the hold. Tie the Sally Mae up at the berth and Walter's crane swings it all into our cold store.
@nell happy: Then you stand at this counter, press {c=#c24a3a}open{/c}, and folk come down the pier asking for things. Give 'em what they ask for and they tip.
@nell smug: And while you're out at sea, I'll sell a bit for you. Friends' rates. Friendly-ish.
@nell happy: Now go and see Walter about the boat! And bring me something {b}chowder-worthy{/b}!
!gesture nell Wave

=== nell_open
@nell happy: Shop's open! Get behind the counter.
!event open_shop

=== nell_upgrades
@nell thinking: Ooh, I've got {b}ideas{/b}. Crushed ice! Lanterns! Bunting! {s}SO much bunting.{/s}
!event upgrades shop
@nell happy: It's looking more like a real shop every day.
-> nell_more

=== nell_junk
!event sell_junk
!if var:junk==0 -> nell_junk_none
@nell happy: {junk} bits of junk! Out of the sea and into the bin, where they belong. Here's {junkpay} sand dollars for the trouble.
@nell smug: Every boot you haul up is a boot a crab doesn't have to live in. Well — some crabs like boots. But still.
-> nell_more

=== nell_junk_none
@nell neutral: You haven't got any junk on you! Old boots, tin cans, tangled nets — bring 'em by and I'll pay a sand dollar or two.
-> nell_more

=== nell_business
!event restoration
!if sales<200 -> nell_business_slow
@nell happy: Business is {w}booming{/w}! Well — burbling. Gently boiling.
@nell neutral: {progress}
-> nell_more

=== nell_business_slow
@nell worried: Slow, so far. We need fish in the trays! Herring, mackerel, crabs — anything with fins or pinchers.
-> nell_more

=== nell_chowder
@nell laugh: Always! Here.
!sfx item_get
@pip happy: Mmm! What's in it?
@nell smug: Secret recipe. [thinking]It's mostly potatoes. [happy]And love. And a {s}suspicious{/s} amount of pepper.
-> nell_more

=== nell_more
? Something else -> nell_hub
? Bye, Nell! -> nell_bye

=== nell_bye
@nell happy: Mind the gulls! They steal.
!gesture nell Wave

=== shop_empty
@nell worried: The cold store's empty, {name}! Nothing to sell yet.
@nell neutral: Take the Sally Mae out, catch something, and tie up at the berth. The crane does the rest.
